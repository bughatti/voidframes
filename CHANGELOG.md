# Changelog

## [1.0.0] — 2026-10-28

First public release.

### Click casting
- Bind a spell, target, focus, menu or assist to left, right, middle, button 4 and button 5, each with Click, Shift, Ctrl or Alt.
- Bindings are saved per character and per spec, and swap when you change spec.
- Default clicks for every class and spec: heals and the cleanse for healers, targeting plus the class's group help (battle rez, Tricks of the Trade, Misdirection, Intervene, Power Infusion, ...) for everyone else. Spell names checked for 12.1.5.
- Bindings editor: type a spell or drag it from the spellbook; clearing a box unbinds it.

### In combat
- Your HoTs and shields on each frame, with timers and stacks, kept visible through fights using Blizzard's own aura display.
- Debuffs on each frame (the ones Blizzard's raid frames show in combat), bordered by dispel type.
- Cleanse button beside each party frame: shows a debuff your cleanse can remove; click to cleanse.
- Group buffs (Mark of the Wild, Arcane Intellect, Fortitude, Battle Shout, Skyfury, Blessing of the Bronze) in a row above each frame.
- Out of combat, your class's group buff shows on anyone missing it.

### Layout
- Party frames and raid frames; raid frames shrink at 10, 20 and 40 players.
- Width, height, frames per row and opacity settings; Alt+drag to move.
- Optional: hide Blizzard's party and raid frames.
